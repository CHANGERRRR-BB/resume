项目Project

线上货运代理
全流程自动化 End-to-end freight-forwarding automation

个人项目，2026Personal project, 2026

面向货运代理实训系统，把跨机构、强单据依赖的业务流程封装成命令行工具。 A CLI tool for a freight-forwarding training system that turns a document-heavy, multi-institution workflow into repeatable commands.

完成出口、进口各 1 单，均跑到节点 500；出口 SOP 12 步，覆盖约 30 类单据。完整流程、代码和脱敏 SOP 在项目档案中。 Completed one export and one import case to node 500; the export SOP has 12 steps and covers around 30 document types. The full flow, code, and sanitized SOP are in the project dossier.
